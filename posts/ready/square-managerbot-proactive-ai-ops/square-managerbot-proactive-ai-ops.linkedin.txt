**Square just shipped an “AI ops manager” for small businesses.**

Managerbot (open beta) sits inside Square Dashboard and *proactively* spots issues, then drafts fixes for you to approve.

Think:
- Inventory gaps before the lunch rush
- Schedule drafts based on projected sales
- Win-back campaigns drafted from real customer behavior
- Catalog cleanup that prevents silent revenue leaks

The key difference vs most “AI assistants”:
It doesn’t wait for you to ask perfect questions. It shows up with a short, actionable to-do list.

If you’re on Square (US, non-franchise food & beverage, retail, health & beauty), Square says it’s currently in open beta at no extra cost.
