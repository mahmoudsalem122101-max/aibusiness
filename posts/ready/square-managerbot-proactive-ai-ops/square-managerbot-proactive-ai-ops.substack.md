Subject: Square’s “AI ops manager” for SMBs

You don’t need more AI. You need fewer fires.

Square’s new Managerbot (now in open beta) is built for exactly that: it watches your sales, labor, inventory, and catalog, then brings you a short to-do list with recommended actions, ready for you to approve.

## A quick scenario
It’s 10:30am. Lunch rush is coming.

Instead of pulling reports, you open Square and see:
- “You’re going to run out of a top-selling item by 1:15pm. Want me to mark it unavailable at Location 2 and suggest a substitute?”
- “You’re overstaffed 2–4pm based on projected sales. Want a draft schedule tweak?”
- “These regulars haven’t been back this month. Want a win-back campaign drafted?”

## What it is
Managerbot is an always-on AI agent built into Square Dashboard.

It monitors your Square data, spots issues/opportunities, and drafts actions (schedule drafts, campaign drafts, item availability updates) for you to approve.

## Best SMB use cases
- Morning performance brief without pulling reports
- Inventory gaps before they hit the customer
- Schedules that start as a draft, not a blank page
- Marketing drafts based on real behavior
- Catalog cleanup (missing photos, duplicate taxes, missing descriptions)

## Pricing/requirements
Square says it’s available in open beta at no additional cost to many US non-franchise food & beverage, retail, and health & beauty sellers (and it’s intended for owners/employees with full access permissions).
