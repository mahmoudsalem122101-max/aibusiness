1/8 Square just shipped something SMBs actually want: an “AI ops manager.”

Managerbot (open beta) lives inside Square Dashboard and brings you a short list of issues + drafted fixes to approve.

2/8 The shift: from reactive chat (“ask me anything”) → proactive ops (“here’s what’s about to break”).

3/8 Use case #1: stockouts before they happen.
It watches sales velocity vs stock and flags likely shortages.

4/8 Use case #2: schedule drafts.
It drafts optimized schedules based on projected sales + team availability, then you review.

5/8 Use case #3: marketing drafts you’ll actually send.
It surfaces campaign opportunities and drafts content for you to edit + approve.

6/8 Use case #4: catalog cleanup.
It flags missing photos, duplicate tax rates, missing descriptions, before customers feel the mess.

7/8 Pricing/availability (per Square): open beta, no additional cost for many US non-franchise food & beverage, retail, health & beauty sellers (eligibility varies).
