**Stop hiring humans to click dashboards.**

A real trend for SMB ops: tool-first AI agents.

Model Context Protocol (MCP) just shipped major updates (OAuth 2.1 auth, streamable HTTP, batching).

Microsoft also released Playwright-MCP, which lets an AI agent reliably:
- navigate pages
- click/type
- read structured accessibility snapshots (not screenshots)

What this means:
You can automate the “web-only” stuff:
- invoice status checks
- weekly KPI pulls
- support ticket tagging
- form → CRM entry

Pricing reality:
Playwright-MCP is open source (no public price). Your cost is the agent/model usage + monitoring.

If you want to try it, start with ONE read-only workflow and add guardrails (least-privilege account, logs, allowlisted domains).
