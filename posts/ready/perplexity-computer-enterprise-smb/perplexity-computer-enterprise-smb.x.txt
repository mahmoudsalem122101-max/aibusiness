1/8 Slack can now be your “ops assistant.”

2/8 The SMB angle: this is for the boring expensive work.
Ticket triage. Draft replies. Weekly summaries. Sales call briefs.

3/8 A Friday 4:45pm prompt:
“@computer triage weekend tickets by severity, draft customer responses, write escalation briefs, and package everything into a Monday standup doc.”

4/8 Why it’s trending: it’s an orchestration layer.
You give an objective → it breaks it into steps → uses models + connectors → ships a finished doc.

5/8 Best starter workflows for 5–200 person teams:
- Support triage + Monday handoff doc
- Sales call prep (CRM + notes)
- Weekly ops update from Slack

6/8 Implementation rule: least privilege.
Use a dedicated service account.
Limit Slack channels.
Read-only where possible.
Approvals for anything that sends emails/changes records.

7/8 Real risks:
- Prompt injection via tickets/emails/web content
- Confident but wrong summaries
- Oversharing if you connect too many apps too fast
