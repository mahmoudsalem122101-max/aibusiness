1/8 Copilot isn’t just writing your emails anymore. Microsoft’s new “Copilot Cowork” is built to *do the work* across Outlook, Teams, Excel, and files (with approval checkpoints).

2/8 The idea: you describe an outcome. Cowork turns it into a plan, runs it in the background, and pulls context from your Microsoft 365 data.

3/8 SMB scenario: “Prep tomorrow’s client meeting.”
Cowork can gather the thread context, build a briefing doc, update a deck, draft the follow-up email, and block prep time.

4/8 Best early use cases for small teams:
• Meeting packets (doc + deck + email)
• Calendar cleanup + focus time
• Account/vendor research with citations
• Launch plans (doc + spreadsheet + assets)

5/8 Why this matters: most SMB ops work is scavenger hunts across inbox + Teams + files. Agents help when they can *connect* those systems, not just chat.

6/8 Practical rollout:
Start with 1 repeatable workflow.
Create a “Cowork-safe” SharePoint folder + templates.
Keep a human approval gate for anything that sends/schedules/edits numbers.

7/8 Risks to watch:
• Messy permissions = accidental over-sharing
• Excel workflows need extra review
• If key info lives in screenshots/DMs, packets will miss it

8/8 It’s in Research Preview + Frontier program right now (Cowork pricing not publicly listed yet). Still worth prepping if you live in M365.
