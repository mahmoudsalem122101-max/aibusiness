Subject: Copilot that actually does the work

Copilot isn’t just writing your emails anymore. Microsoft’s new Copilot Cowork is an “agent mode” that can plan and execute multi-step tasks across Outlook, Teams, Excel, and your files, with approval checkpoints.

## A quick scenario

It’s 4:40pm. A client moved tomorrow’s call to 9:00am. You have decisions buried in Outlook threads, updates floating in Teams, an Excel pipeline in SharePoint, and a deck from last time that needs changes.

Cowork’s pitch is simple: you hand it the outcome (“prep the meeting packet, update the deck with the latest numbers, draft the follow-up, and block 20 minutes of prep time”), and it does the digging and drafting across Microsoft 365, then asks you to approve actions before changes land.

## What it is (plain English)

Copilot Cowork is Microsoft 365 Copilot’s new execution layer:
- turns your ask into a plan
- runs the plan in the background
- grounds work in your Microsoft 365 context (email, calendar, Teams, files)
- proposes actions with checkpoints and approvals

Microsoft says it runs within Microsoft 365’s security and governance boundaries, and actions/outputs are auditable.

## The top SMB use cases

- Calendar cleanup and focus time protection
- Client meeting packets (briefing doc + updated deck + follow-up email)
- Fast account/vendor research with citations
- Launch/campaign coordination (docs + spreadsheets + assignments)

## Pricing and availability (reality check)

Cowork isn’t a standalone SMB tool you buy today. It’s part of Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Microsoft says it’s in Research Preview with broader availability through the Frontier program. Cowork-specific pricing isn’t publicly listed in the announcement materials.

## A safe rollout for small teams

1) Pick one workflow with a clear before/after (meeting packets is the best)
2) Tighten Microsoft 365 permissions (SharePoint, Teams guests, shared mailbox delegates)
3) Create a “Cowork-safe” working set (one SharePoint site + templates)
4) Use approval gates aggressively (especially for sending/scheduling and Excel)
5) Define “done” so output is repeatable, not vibes

## Risks and limits

- Messy workspace = missing or wrong summaries
- Excel workflows need extra review
- Sloppy permissions can cause accidental over-sharing
- It’s preview/frontier right now, so timing may vary

If your SMB runs on Microsoft 365, Cowork is one of the more practical “agent” launches we’ve seen, because it lives where your work already is.
