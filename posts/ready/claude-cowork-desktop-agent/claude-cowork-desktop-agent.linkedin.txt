**Your desktop just got an AI “operator”, not a chatbot.**

Anthropic’s Claude Cowork can take a folder of messy files (receipts, notes, exports) and hand back real deliverables: a clean spreadsheet, a branded report draft, a tidy handoff packet.

SMB-friendly wins:
- Receipt screenshots → categorized expense sheet
- Scattered notes → 1-page weekly recap
- Downloads chaos → organized, renamed folders
- Template + exports → first-draft reports/decks

The trick: roll it out like an employee.
Give it ONE sandbox folder, start with boring tasks, and require human review for anything customer-facing or financial.
