**AI automations don’t fail because your prompt was bad. They fail because the workflow dies halfway through.**

If you run an SMB, you’ve seen it:
- the “agent” times out
- a token expires
- it restarts and duplicates work
- nobody can tell what happened

Mistral just launched *Workflows* (public preview), built on Temporal, to make multi-step AI automations durable:
- retries that actually resume
- an audit trail per step
- built-in human approvals (pause, review, continue)

Practical SMB use cases:
- inbox triage + draft replies (with approval gates)
- support ticket routing with traceability
- quote → invoice → follow-up sequences
- recurring daily ops runs (cron-style)

My favorite part is the mindset shift: treat AI like production ops, not a demo.
