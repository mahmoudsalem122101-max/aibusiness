Subject: A 24/7 AI operator for SMBs?

Perplexity is trying to move AI past “chat” into an always-on “digital proxy” that can work across your apps. If it works as advertised, this is one of the first mainstream AI products that could *actually run the busywork* while you sleep.

## The practical SMB angle
Don’t buy this for hype. Buy it if you have repeatable, annoying workflows across email, Slack, CRM, and spreadsheets, and you’re willing to set strict guardrails.

### Where it can pay off fast
- Daily operator brief (sales, support backlog, refunds, ad spend)
- Sales follow-up drafting + CRM task cleanup
- Support triage and reply drafting (with approval)
- Weekly “what changed” reporting from your dashboards

## Pricing (what we can verify)
Reporting we can access says “Personal Computer” requires Perplexity Max at $200/month and is currently waitlist-based.

## How to try it without risking your business
1) Pilot ONE workflow for two weeks.
2) Use a dedicated agent account (not your personal admin login).
3) Start draft-only: no sending emails, no changing customer records.
4) Restrict access to one folder/channel.
5) Measure hours saved and dropped balls prevented.
