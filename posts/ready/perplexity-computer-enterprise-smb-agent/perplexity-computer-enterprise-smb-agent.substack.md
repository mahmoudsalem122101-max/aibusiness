Perplexity just turned “ask in Slack” into “get the work done”, with an enterprise version of its AI agent called **Computer**.

If you run a small team, the interesting part is not the tech. It’s this: **you can route real operations work (ticket triage, proposal drafts, weekly reporting, account research) through a Slack-native agent that can pull from your tools, not just the public web.**

## A quick scenario (because this is how it actually shows up)
It’s Monday morning. Your support inbox is messy. Sales wants a quick proposal tweak. Someone needs a “what changed this week” summary for the team meeting.

Instead of five people context switching across Gmail, Notion, HubSpot/Salesforce, and spreadsheets, you drop one message in Slack:

> “Triage weekend tickets by severity, draft replies, and package a Monday standup doc with escalations.”

## What it is (plain English)
**Perplexity Computer for Enterprise** is an AI “orchestration” product that tries to complete multi-step work for you, not just answer questions.

## Top SMB use cases (start here)
- Support triage and response drafting
- Sales call and account prep
- Weekly ops reporting you actually ship
- Proposal and SOW drafting (first draft, not final)
- “Find the answer in our stuff” without you becoming the human search engine

## Pricing and requirements (what we can and can’t confirm)
From accessible sources in this run:
- Coverage describes the enterprise offering as **usage-based**, using an **organization-wide credit pool**.
- **Pricing is not publicly listed in a fetchable official source here**, so expect “talk to sales” dynamics.

## Implementation steps (least-privilege first)
1) Pick one workflow, one team, one channel
2) Lock down connector access (start read-only)
3) Define approval moments (email/CRM/billing)
4) Give it templates that match your business
5) Measure one metric for 2 weeks

## Risks and limits
- Confidential info leakage in shared Slack channels
- Connector overreach (too-broad access)
- Confident wrong outputs (keep high-stakes work in draft mode)
- Spend creep if credits aren’t monitored

## The takeaway
Don’t start by asking it to “run the business.” Start by making it your **operations assistant** for one repeatable workflow, with tight permissions and clear approval rules.
