1/8 Notion just shipped “Custom Agents,” and it’s one of the first agent features that feels *SMB-useful* (not sci-fi).

2/8 Think: a background teammate that routes requests, answers repeat questions, and posts updates across Notion + connected tools.

3/8 Best first win for most teams: Slack request → Notion database item → assigns owner → asks 1 clarifying question → posts tracking link back.

4/8 Other solid use cases:
- weekly ops report posted on schedule
- SOP/policy Q&A in Slack (always cite the source page)
- “where’s the latest doc?” sales enablement

5/8 Pricing is usage-based.
Notion’s pricing page: Custom Agents are “$10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits” (after the free trial/beta window).

6/8 How to pilot safely:
- 1 workflow, 1 channel, 1 owner
- least-privilege access (agent Teamspace, read-only SOPs, edit only the queue)
- approvals for anything external/customer-facing

7/8 Real risks:
- prompt injection (content tries to manipulate the agent)
- confidently wrong answers if your SOPs are messy
- cost creep if it runs all day in busy channels

8/8 If you already run your ops in Notion + Slack, this is worth a 7-day controlled test.
