1/8 Stop rewriting the same customer emails.
Gmail’s new “contextual Smart Replies” can draft a real reply using the *whole thread*, not just a generic one-liner.

2/8 Who it’s for:
SMBs living in Gmail (support@, sales@, ops) where questions repeat and speed matters.

3/8 What it does (plain English):
When you hit Reply, Gmail shows 2–3 longer suggested replies that pull details from the conversation.
You click, edit, send.

4/8 Best use cases:
• Support follow-ups (status, next steps)
• Sales back-and-forth (recaps, scheduling)
• Project updates (scope/timeline confirmations)
• Vendor coordination

5/8 What it doesn’t do well:
• Anything not already in the thread
• Legal/HR/medical topics
• Disputes/refunds beyond policy
(You still own the send button.)

6/8 Pricing/requirements:
It’s part of Google Workspace plans that list “Gemini in Gmail.” Pricing depends on your Workspace tier (per-user/month).

7/8 Rollout in 30 minutes:
• Pilot 1 inbox
• Rule: AI drafts fast, humans verify facts + tone
• Add a tiny “house style” snippet doc
• Decide what never gets AI help

8/8 The win: faster replies without sounding like robots.
