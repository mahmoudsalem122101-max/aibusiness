**Agents that create agents is trending. Here’s the SMB takeaway.**

A new wave of “agentic automation” is getting noisy again.

Emergence AI is being covered for a platform where you describe a task, and the system can generate new AI agents to do it.

Cool demo.
But the real business value is the pattern:

• A registry of approved tools/connectors
• Verification before action (esp. money, CRM, comms)
• Human checkpoints for high-risk steps
• Reusable templates and audit logs

If you’re an entrepreneur, don’t chase autonomy.
Chase reliability.

Practical first wins:
• Support triage that drafts replies (humans send)
• Sales ops that proposes CRM updates (humans approve)
• Invoice follow-ups that queue drafts, not emails

Pricing note: pricing isn’t publicly listed (enterprise contact).
