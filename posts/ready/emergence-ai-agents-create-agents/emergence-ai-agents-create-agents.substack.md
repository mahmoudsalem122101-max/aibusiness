Subject: Agents that create agents (SMB reality check)

Agents that create agents is trending again.

Emergence AI is getting fresh coverage for a platform where you describe a task in plain English, and the system can generate new AI agents to complete it. The promise is dynamic automation, where workflows are assembled on demand instead of being manually scripted.

Here’s the useful part for entrepreneurs and SMBs.

## What’s actually new (in plain terms)
The platform checks a registry of existing agents and, if it can’t solve the task, it generates a new agent, registers it, and uses it. The big idea is not “full autonomy”. It’s *dynamic assembly plus verification*.

## The SMB play: copy the pattern, not the hype
If you want practical ROI, build around these mechanics:

1) A small tool registry
Give your AI a catalog of safe, testable tools (read-only first, safe-write second, high-risk always needs approval).

2) Verification before action
Validate schemas, apply policies, and require evidence from your own records before anything gets written to CRM or sent to customers.

3) Human checkpoints
Anything that touches money, customer communications, or irreversible changes should be “propose, then approve”.

## Best first automations
- Support triage that drafts replies (humans send)
- Sales ops that proposes CRM updates (humans approve)
- Invoice follow-ups that queue drafts, not automatic emails
- Weekly KPI agent that shows filters and assumptions
